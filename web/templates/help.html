How to play

Two players, seven columns, six rows — first to connect four wins

Goal

Line up four of your discs in a row — across, up and down, or diagonally. If the board fills with no winner, the game is a tie.

Dropping discs

X goes first. Tap a column number (1–7) to drop a disc into that column. It falls to the lowest open slot. You cannot play in a full column.

Hot seat

Pass the device after each move. The status line shows whose turn it is and when the game ends.
